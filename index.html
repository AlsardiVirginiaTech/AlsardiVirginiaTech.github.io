Research

My work centers on simulating earthquake-triggered slope and landslide runout using higher-order formulations of the Material Point Method (MPM), including isogeometric analysis and multiphase hydromechanical coupling. Most of this research lives inside Anura3D, an open-source MPM code I help develop and maintain.

currently: PM4Sand + higher-order MPM for liquefaction-induced runout
